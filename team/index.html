Founder & Leader

Robert Prokop

CEO & Founder

Born December 7, 1967 in Östersund, Sweden, Robert is a seasoned civil and structural engineer with over two decades of global experience in the design and construction of complex infrastructure, particularly in the energy sector.

He earned his B.Sc. in Civil Engineering from the University of Liverpool and an M.Sc. in Structural Engineering from Nova Southeastern University in Florida. As CEO of Wexmoor Builders, Robert leads with purpose, precision, and people-first thinking.

Read Full Biography

Raised in a humble Swedish household, Robert spent his formative years fascinated by how things were built — from bridges to barns. This passion grew into a lifelong pursuit of engineering excellence.

Over the years, Robert has overseen the engineering and construction of major gas facilities across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and parts of Asia. His work has contributed to energy infrastructure supporting millions of people.

He is also a passionate advocate for young engineers, mentoring future professionals and championing STEM education in underserved areas. A world traveller who has visited over 20 countries, Robert credits his international experiences with providing a broader perspective on human needs and the transformative power of well-designed infrastructure.
